Things people ask

My stints didn't sync

Stint works with no connection: your stints count and go up when there's signal again. If a score is still missing after a minute online, open the app and pull the Leaderboard.

I ended a stint early. Did I lose my streak?

No. You lose the points for that one stint, nothing else. A streak is about days you finished something, and tomorrow is a new day.

Somebody in my circle is being unpleasant

Tap the ⋯ next to their name and choose Report, or report the whole circle from the Circle tab. Whoever started the circle can remove someone and reset the invite code at the same time, so they can't rejoin with the old one. If somebody is in danger, tell an adult you trust.

How do I get my data, or delete it?

In the app: You → Privacy & data. Download my data gives you a readable file of everything we hold. Delete account removes all of it.

Why does Stint need Screen Time?

It's how Stint pauses the apps you pick while a stint runs, and that's the one thing Stint is for, so it asks you to connect before anything else. Which apps you pick stays on your phone: Apple gives Stint codes it can't read, so Stint can't tell which apps they are.

Stint says it can't connect to Screen Time

Tap Try again. If iPhone doesn't ask again, open Settings, go to Screen Time and check Stint is allowed. Screen Time also needs a passcode set on your phone. If someone else set up Screen Time on your phone, they can check those settings with you.

My stint ended by itself

If Screen Time access was turned off while a stint was running, Stint couldn't keep your apps paused, so that stint ended early and doesn't count. Connect again and start another whenever you like.
