Draft for the TestFlight beta. Last updated 3 October 2026.

This is a draft. NOTHING HERE IS LEGAL ADVICE.

What Stint is

An app for doing focused work in short stretches, with a few friends who can see how much time you put in. It is not a parental control app, and it never reports to anyone but the circle you chose.

Stint works with Screen Time: while a stint runs, it pauses the apps you picked. It needs Screen Time access to do that, so without it you can't start stints, see the leaderboard or use your circles. You can still sign out, delete your account, and read these terms and the privacy policy.

Who it's for

People 13 and over. If you're younger, Stint isn't for you yet. This is a beta for a small group of testers, so expect the occasional rough edge.

Your part of the deal

  • Be kind in a circle. Your name and emoji are the only things others see of you. Use them to be someone people recognise, not to have a go at anyone.
  • Only share an invite code with people you actually know. A code is the key to a circle. Anyone who has it can join until the code is reset.
  • Don't try to move people off Stint. Names can't carry links, phone numbers or handles, and we check.
  • Report what's wrong. There's a Report button on every member and every circle. If somebody is in danger, tell an adult you trust, not just us.

Our part

  • Your stints are yours. Screen Time data never leaves your phone.
  • No ads. Nothing about you is sold.
  • You can take your data with you, or delete all of it, from inside the app.
  • If we suspend or remove an account for hurting other people, we'll say why if asked.

What we don't promise

This is a beta run by one person. Stint may be unavailable, may lose a setting, or may change. Don't rely on it for anything that matters more than a study session. It's provided as is, without warranties, and to the extent the law allows we aren't liable for losses arising from using it.

Pausing apps is done by Apple's Screen Time, so it works as well as Screen Time does. If Screen Time access is turned off during a stint, that stint ends early and doesn't count. If Stint is closed during a stint, the apps stay paused until you open Stint again.

Ending it

You can delete your account in the app at any time. We can end the beta, or close an account that's being used to hurt people, and we'll email you if we do.
