Focus time, with your friends

Stint is a timer you start on purpose. Pick how long, hold the ring, and the apps you chose stay out of the way until it's done. A few friends see how much time you put in this week. That's the whole idea.

What it isn't

It isn't parental control. Nobody is watching you, nothing is reported to a grown-up, and every stint is one you chose to start. Ending one early is always allowed: you just don't get the points for it.

What stays on your phone

Stint uses Screen Time to pause the apps you pick, so it asks for Screen Time access first. Stint can't see which apps those are or how you use your phone, and neither can your circle. What leaves your phone is a name, an emoji, and how many minutes you finished.

The privacy policy says it properly.

Stint is in a closed beta for a small group of testers, and is for people 13 and over.
